JAPANESE VOCAB VOICES (sounds/jp/<word>_f.mp3 female, _m.mp3 male)
24 World-1 words × 2 voices, to match each NPC's gender. Female = macOS TTS Kyoko (ja_JP). Male =
the same Kyoko clip pitch-shifted down ~5 semitones (the only ja_JP voice actually installed here;
Otoya/Hattori show in the voice list but their data isn't downloaded, so they output silence).
Pronunciation is identical/correct — only the pitch differs. For a true male voice, install Otoya
via System Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > System Voices, then regenerate _m clips.
All normalized to -15 LUFS mono.
Plus 24 FOOD words (sounds/jp/<food>_f.mp3, female only — the hero is a girl) spoken on eat, same
Kyoko TTS, -15 LUFS mono. Food rotates through all 24 so kids hear a new food word each pickup.
Plus 20 ENEMY words (sounds/jp/<enemy>_m.mp3, male voice — monsters) spoken on defeat: real
animal/monster words (hebi/snake, kani/crab, kurage/jellyfish, doragon/dragon, robotto/robot…).
NOTE: macOS system-voice output is fine for prototype/demo; for a commercial launch, regenerate
these with a TTS that explicitly licenses commercial use of the audio (Google Cloud TTS, Amazon
Polly, or ElevenLabs) to be airtight.
