# KAIJU CLASH — SOUND RULES (read this, make good sound)

Hi. You make sound for our game. Kids 8+ play it. It runs in a web browser, mostly on PHONES.
You can **record** new sound OR **find/buy** ready-made sound. **I do not care which.** I care that
it is GOOD and CLEAN and fits the game. Both ways must pass the same test below.

If sound is bad, we do not use it. Simple.

---

## THE GAME (so you make the right vibe)
- Cute kaiju (baby Godzilla-type monsters) stomp a Japanese city, save little people, eat food, fart.
- Look = bright **16-bit pixel art**, happy, Japan, SNES/arcade feeling.
- So sound = **fun, bouncy, cartoony, cute. Arcade / chiptune / 16-bit flavor.** Japan flavor good.
- **NOT** scary. **NOT** dark. **NOT** harsh. **NOT** realistic gore. Kids must smile.

---

## CLEAN = THE #1 RULE
A sound passes only if it is CLEAN:
- **No hiss.** No background hum. No room noise. No air conditioner. No computer fan.
- **No clicks. No pops.** No crackle.
- **No clipping. No distortion.** (Red on the meter = bad. Fix it.)
- **No echo / no reverb tail** you did not want.
- **No silence at the start.** Sound must fire THE INSTANT it plays. Trim the dead air off the front.
- **No other game's famous sound.** No Mario coin, no Zelda chime, no movie clips. Original or library only.

If you RECORD: quiet room, good mic, no echo, clean edit, normalize.
If you FIND/BUY: must already be clean studio quality. No watermark voice ("freesound dot com..."). 

---

## THE NUMBERS (match what we already have)
| Thing | Spec |
|---|---|
| File type | **MP3** |
| Sample rate | **44100 Hz (44.1 kHz)** |
| Sound effects (SFX) | **MONO**, ~96 kbps |
| Music | **STEREO**, ~128 kbps |
| Loudness | Normalize. Loud and punchy but **never clipping**. Aim ~ **-14 LUFS**. |
| SFX length | **SHORT. Snappy.** Most under 1 second. Instant. |
| Music length | A loop ~1–2 minutes that **LOOPS SEAMLESS** (see below) |

---

## SFX = SHORT AND PUNCHY
- A stomp, a jump, a pop, a chomp, a fart, a coin, a hit. These happen FAST and OFTEN.
- Make them **short, tight, juicy**. Player taps button → sound NOW. No delay.
- **All SFX must feel like ONE family** — same loudness, same clean room/library. Not one quiet + one screaming.
- Phone speakers are TINY and weak. Put the energy in the **MID** range so it cuts through a phone.
  Do **not** rely on deep boom/sub-bass — phones can't play it, you just hear nothing.

## MUSIC = LOOPS WITH NO GAP
- Theme music plays over and over while kids play. **The loop point must be INVISIBLE.**
- No little silence at the end. No click/pop where it restarts. Cut it on the beat so end → start is smooth.
- Music sits **UNDER** the SFX. Make it a touch quieter than the SFX so stomps/pops sit on top.
- Each world wants its own flavor (City day = bright/happy. Neon night = cool/funky. Boss = big/tense-but-fun).
