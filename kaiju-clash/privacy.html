🦖 Kaiju Kids — Privacy Policy

Effective: June 29, 2026 · For the Kaiju Kids game (kaijukids.co and the Kaiju Kids iOS app)
Short version for parents: Kaiju Kids is made for children. It does not ask for or collect real names, emails, phone numbers, location, photos, or contacts. There are no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics. The only thing that ever leaves the device is an optional, made-up nickname and game scores — used only to show leaderboards — and only after a grown-up passes a parental check.

Who we are

Kaiju Kids is published by AurionX. Questions or requests? Email privacy@kaijukids.co.

What we collect — and what we don't

The game works fully without any account. If a player chooses to use the optional Leaderboards, and a grown-up passes the parental check, the app creates a play account that stores:

  • A made-up nickname — randomly generated by the game (for example, SaltyStomp60). Players cannot type their own name, so a real name is never collected.
  • A 6-letter friend code — so friends can compare scores. It is not linked to any real-world identity.
  • Game scores and learning progress — best level times and Japanese-word mastery points, to show on the leaderboards.

We do not collect: real names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, precise or coarse location, photos, camera/microphone data, contacts, or device advertising identifiers.

What we never do

  • No third-party advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of any data with third parties.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites (Apple App Tracking Transparency is not used).

How information is used

The nickname, friend code, and scores are used only to operate the leaderboards and friend comparisons inside the game. Game progress and settings are saved on the device itself.

Where it's stored

Leaderboard data is stored on our own servers (hosted on Railway). It is not shared with advertisers or data brokers. Device-side progress stays in the app's local storage on the device.

Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Kaiju Kids is designed for children and complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar rules. Because the only personal-style field — a nickname — is randomly generated rather than typed, and account creation sits behind a parental check, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not condition play on providing personal information.

Parental choices & data deletion

Parents can request that we delete a play account's data at any time. Email privacy@kaijukids.co with the in-game friend code (shown on the Leaderboards screen) and we will delete the associated nickname, code, and scores. Removing the app also clears all device-side data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version here.
