What visual style for the game?

Pick the art direction that feels right for your Godzilla fighter

🦖
PIXEL ART · RETRO

Pixel Art / Retro

Classic 16-bit sprite style. Bold colors, chunky pixels, nostalgic feel. Think Street Fighter II meets Godzilla. Fast to build, works great at any resolution.

🦕
2.5D · NEON GLOW

2.5D Neon / Cinematic

Dark atmospheric backgrounds, glowing energy effects, detailed 2D character sprites with depth. Modern mobile fighter look — like Injustice or MARVEL Contest of Champions lite.

🦎
3D LOW-POLY · CARTOON

3D Low-Poly / Cartoon

Stylized 3D monsters and cities with chunky geometry. Fun, colorful, and instantly readable on small screens. Think mobile Clash of Clans energy but for kaiju.

🦖
REALISTIC DARK · CINEMATIC

Realistic / Dark Cinematic

High-detail textures, dark moody atmosphere, realistic destruction physics. Closest to the actual Godzilla films. Most complex to build, highest visual impact.
