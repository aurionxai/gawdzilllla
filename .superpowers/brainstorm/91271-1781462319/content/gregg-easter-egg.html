🕵️ Who Is Gregg?

🧍
GREGG
1/500 chance
to spawn per stage
SIZE
Tiny — barely visible. About the height of a regular NPC but wearing a little polo shirt and khakis. Has a briefcase.
BEHAVIOR
Wanders aimlessly toward food power-ups. If a kaiju is near food, Gregg runs over and eats it first — stealing the power-up and doing absolutely nothing useful with it.
INVINCIBLE
Cannot be splatted. Kaiju step on him, he bounces, stands back up, brushes off his polo, and keeps walking. He is untouchable. He simply does not care.
