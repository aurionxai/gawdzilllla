🎮 Core Philosophy: Fun First, Never Pay-to-Win

✅ YOU CAN BUY
Cosmetic skins, new characters, city stages, fart sound packs, Gregg voice packs, battle pass tiers, emotes
❌ NEVER FOR SALE
Stat boosts, extra health, more power meter, stronger attacks, P2W advantages of any kind
