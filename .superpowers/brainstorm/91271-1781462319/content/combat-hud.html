⚔️ Attack System

👊 LIGHT ATTACK
Fast claw swipe or punch. Low damage, quick recovery. Tap rapidly for 3-hit combo. Builds power meter +5%.
💥 HEAVY ATTACK
Tail slam, body crush, or ground pound. High damage, slow recovery. Stuns enemy briefly. Builds power meter +15%.
🌀 SPECIAL MOVE
Character-specific signature attack. Costs 25% power. Godzilla = Atomic Breath beam. Kong = Axe slam. Mothra = Wing beam burst.
☢️ UNLEASH (Ultimate)
Full power meter required. 3-second cinematic animation. Massive AoE damage. "GAWDZILLLLA!" voice trigger. Destroys half the screen.
🤸 DODGE
Swipe left/right to dodge. Brief invincibility frames. Perfect dodge (< 0.3s before hit) = COUNTER state — next attack does 2x damage.
🫂 GRAB + THROW
Hold HEAVY near enemy → grab. Swipe direction to throw into buildings. Buildings collapse = bonus destruction + NPC splats.

🏙️ Story/Smash Mode HUD Differences

Destruction Score
Replaces opponent health bar. Dollar value of damage done — buildings, vehicles, NPCs all have values. Target score to advance.
NPC Splat Combo
Stomp 5 NPCs in 2 seconds = SPLAT STREAK. Text pops: "SQUISH x5 — GAWDZILLLLA APPROVES." Multiplier to score.
Military Wave Counter
Top of screen shows incoming wave: tanks → jets → helicopters → boss kaiju. Countdown timer per wave.
City Destruction %
Progress bar showing how much of the city is rubble. Hit 100% = stage clear bonus + next city unlocked.
