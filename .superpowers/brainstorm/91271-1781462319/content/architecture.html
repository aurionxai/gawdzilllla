🗺️ Game Modes

⚔️ VS Mode
Pick 2 fighters — human or AI. Fight until one health bar hits zero. Local 2P on same device.
🏙️ Story / Smash Mode
Pick a kaiju or hero. Rampage through cities — smash buildings, splat NPCs, hit destruction score targets. Boss fights mid-stage.
🌊 Survival Mode
Endless waves of military NPCs + enemy kaiju. How long can you last? Leaderboard score.
🏆 Tournament
Single-elimination bracket vs. CPU. Win to unlock lore + new characters. Tied to battle pass seasons.
