How A+B work together

  • Evolution Tree sets your form — visual appearance in FPS, unique abilities locked to that form, and which stats get bonuses
  • Stats grow within any form — spend points each level, food boosts specific stats permanently
  • Destroy vs. spare choices push you toward Burning or Armored branch at Lv12
  • Each city conquered gives XP + one evolution point toward the next form
