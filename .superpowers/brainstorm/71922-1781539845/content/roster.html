RODAN
Air mobility specialist. Supersonic dive closes distance instantly. Sonic Screech stuns multiple enemies. Talon Grab picks up enemy titans and drops them away from the city.

KONG
Melee powerhouse. Throw Debris gives him ranged option. Ground Slam shakes an area — knocks back enemies AND redirects falling rubble away from civilians. His hands save buildings.
