A — Linear Story

Titan attacks escalate city to city in a fixed order. Beat Tokyo → Seoul unlocks. Each city has a scripted threat and story beat. The restaurant cinema and food reward come at the end of each. Powers unlock at story milestones.

B — World Map (Recommended)

After each city, 2–3 new attacks appear on the globe. You choose which crisis to respond to next. Story still has a main thread (Monarch updates you on the big villain) but you pick the order. Unlock new cities by defeating enough titans in a region.

C — Acts / Chapters

Cities grouped into 4 acts by region (Pacific, Eurasia, Americas, Final). Beat all cities in an act to unlock the next. Each act ends with a major boss titan. Clear story structure — feels like a movie in chapters.
